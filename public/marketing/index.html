Who is Ear to Mind for?+

Product designers doing research in real projects. It is a space to practice non-directed interviewing, cognitive statement writing, and mental model mapping—whether you work alone or with a note-taker.

Will it write my statements for me?+

No. The coaching approach keeps the thinking with you. You write, revise, and group your own statements. Guidance helps you notice what to explore without supplying a finished answer.

What can I do in the workspace?+

Create research projects, import interview notes, save quotes, revise cognitive statements, and build a mental model map. Connect the personal plugin to receive coaching through your ChatGPT conversation. Audio recording and live transcription are not included.

Where does my work go?+

Your signed-in workspace saves research to your private account. You choose when to prepare a ChatGPT review. Export projects at any time, or delete a project and its coaching history from project settings.

What is the method behind it?+

The product is informed by Indi Young’s mental model method and non-directed interviews. It uses four lenses—Action, Thought, Feeling, and Philosophy—to help you look beyond surface behavior. This is an independent learning tool.
